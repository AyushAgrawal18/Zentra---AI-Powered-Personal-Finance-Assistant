# 1. Purpose

This document outlines the planned evolution of Zentra beyond Version 1.0.0.

It helps guide future development while keeping the Version 1 scope focused and achievable.

Items listed here are ideas and planned enhancements. They are **not part of the MVP** unless explicitly stated elsewhere.

---

# 2. Vision

Zentra aims to become an intelligent personal finance platform that not only records financial activity but also helps users make smarter financial decisions.

The long-term vision is to provide:

- Complete financial visibility
- Intelligent financial recommendations
- Simple payment experiences
- Automated financial tracking
- Cross-platform accessibility
- Secure and scalable infrastructure

# 6. Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is to transform Zentra into a complete financial ecosystem.

Potential capabilities include:

- Open Banking Integrations
- Insurance Tracking
- Loan Management
- Credit Score Insights
- Financial Health Score
- Retirement Planning
- Tax Planning
- Wealth Dashboard

---

# 7. AI Roadmap

Future AI capabilities may include:

- Personalized Financial Coach
- Voice-Based AI Assistant
- Smart Spending Predictions
- Automatic Budget Planning
- Goal Achievement Forecasts
- Fraud Detection Assistance
- Personalized Savings Strategies
- Weekly Financial Reports

The AI layer should remain provider-independent through the existing provider abstraction architecture.

# 9. Infrastructure Roadmap

Future infrastructure improvements:

- Redis Caching
- Background Workers
- Queue Processing
- Object Storage
- CDN
- Monitoring Dashboard
- Centralized Logging
- Read Replicas
- Horizontal Scaling

---

# 10. Out of Scope

The following are intentionally excluded from Version 1:

- Direct UPI Processing
- Net Banking
- Credit Card Processing
- Cryptocurrency
- International Payments
- Multi-Currency Support
- Investment Trading
- Stock Market Integration
- Loan Applications

These features may be evaluated in future releases.

---

# 11. Success Vision

Zentra will be considered successful when it becomes:

- Easy to use
- Fast and responsive
- Secure by default
- Helpful through AI
- Reliable for everyday finance management
- Scalable for future growth
- Simple to maintain
- Well documented

The long-term objective is to build a platform that users trust for managing their daily financial life while providing developers with a clean, scalable, and maintainable architecture.

---

# Guiding Principles

As Zentra evolves:

- Keep the architecture modular.
- Preserve backward compatibility whenever possible.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.
- Prioritize user privacy and security.
- Expand features without compromising performance.
- Continue documenting every major architectural and product decision.

---

> **Future Scope Principle:** Version 1 should solve the core problem exceptionally well. Every future enhancement should build on a stable foundation without increasing unnecessary complexity.# 🔮 Future Scope
